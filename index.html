YOUR DATA

Privacy and storage

Your schedule: saved in this browser’s local storage. ClearDay does not upload it to the GitHub repository or an app server.

Calendar access: imported only when you press Sync. ClearDay asks for busy times, not titles or notes. OAuth tokens are handled by Google in memory or Microsoft’s MSAL browser cache.

Backups: downloaded JSON contains your schedule and may include private calendar event IDs. Keep it somewhere private. Do not share it or put it in GitHub.

Disconnect: removes imported busy blocks and saved client IDs from this browser. For full revocation, also remove ClearDay from your Google Account or Microsoft account permissions.

Delete schedule: permanently removes this browser’s schedule. Download a backup first if you may need it later.
